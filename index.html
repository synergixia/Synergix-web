HOW THE AI ANSWERS

The RAG engine is what makes Synergix fundamentally different. It doesn't guess — it retrieves from the blockchain first.

1
Question arrives — user asks anything in the Telegram bot in any of the 4 languages.
2
Brain loaded — the latest SYNERGIXAI brain version is fetched from the Greenfield bucket. This is the fused collective knowledge.
3
Contributions searched — the RAG engine scans all indexed on-chain contributions using multi-factor scoring: quality × impact × author rank × recency × language match.
4
80/20 construction — if relevant data is found in the bucket, the AI uses 80% community data + 20% inference. If nothing relevant: 100% inference with an explicit notice.
5
Authors rewarded — every contribution used in a response triggers automatic residual point awards to authors. No action needed. The blockchain records it.
6
Knowledge grows — the federation loop fuses all contributions every 8 minutes into a new brain version, uploaded to Greenfield, making the AI smarter with every cycle.
// THE 80/20 RULE
80%
From the Greenfield Bucket
When the RAG finds relevant contributions on-chain, the AI builds 80% of the response from those immutable, community-validated, blockchain-stored records. This is not AI hallucination — this is immutable fact.
20%
AI Context & Inference
The remaining 20% adds context, structure and clarification. It never overrides the community data. The bucket is the primary source. The AI is the translator.
